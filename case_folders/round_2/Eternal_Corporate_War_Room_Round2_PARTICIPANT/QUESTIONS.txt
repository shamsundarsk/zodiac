ROUND 2 - CORPORATE WAR ROOM

This archive is a business-performance investigation. Reconstruct financial performance, competition, spending, investment effectiveness, operations, customers and internal risk.

The detailed internal figures are synthetic game data. You are expected to calculate, filter, reconcile and cross-reference.

1. Calculate the FY23-FY26 revenue CAGR. Give one decimal place.
2. From FY25 to FY26, by how many percentage points did normalized operating margin change? Which expenditure category had the largest absolute increase?
3. Across FY24-Q1 to FY26-Q4, which competitor gained the most market share, and by how many percentage points?
4. In FY26-Q3 pricing, which segment has the widest contract-value premium versus a tracked competitor, and which competitor event matches that segment?
5. Calculate the percentage increase in Digital expenditure from FY25 to FY26. Which project is linked to the digital-platform procurement trail?
6. Which strategic project consumed the largest share of strategic-project actual spend? Calculate its portfolio percentage.
7. Trace the specialist component payment: transaction ID, vendor ID, PO number, project code, source country and receiving site.
8. Which strategic project has the largest expected-vs-actual ROI gap? State expected ROI, actual ROI and gap in percentage points.
9. Which PX-GAMMA site has utilization below 60%, inventory days above 60 and energy cost above INR 1.45 per unit? What percentage of the modeled total site capacity does it represent?
10. What percentage of strategic-account revenue is contributed by the top five accounts, and which top-five account has the largest retention decline?
11. Which product segment has the widest gross-to-contribution margin gap, and which cost component contributes the most?
12. Produce three measurable business weaknesses supported by at least four files across at least three folders. For each, provide a metric, its change/variance and an evidence path.
