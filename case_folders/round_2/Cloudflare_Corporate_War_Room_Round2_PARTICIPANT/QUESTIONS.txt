ROUND 2 - CORPORATE WAR ROOM

Case: Cloudflare business-performance investigation
Objective: reconstruct performance, competition, capital allocation, network economics, customer risk and operational constraints.
Important: the detailed internal figures are synthetic game data. Public figures are benchmark/context only. Calculate, filter, reconcile and cross-reference.

1. Calculate the FY23-FY26 revenue CAGR from the financial model. Give the answer to one decimal place.
2. From FY25 to FY26, by how many percentage points did normalized operating margin change? Which expenditure category had the largest absolute increase over the same period?
3. Across FY24-Q1 to FY26-Q4, which competitor gained the most market share, and by how many percentage points?
4. In FY26-Q3 pricing, which segment has the widest Cloudflare contract-value premium against a tracked competitor, and which competitor event matches that segment?
5. Calculate the percentage increase in Transit & Peering expenditure from FY25 to FY26. Which strategic project is directly connected to the buried procurement trail for this spend?
6. Which strategic project consumed the largest share of the strategic-project actual-spend portfolio? Calculate the percentage.
7. Trace the specialist-equipment payment and identify the transaction ID, vendor ID, PO number, project code, source country and receiving site.
8. Which strategic project has the largest expected-vs-actual ROI gap? State expected ROI, actual ROI and the gap in percentage points.
9. Which ZT-22 site has utilization below 60%, transit cost above USD 1.45/TB and latency above 65 ms? What percentage of modeled total site capacity does it represent?
10. What percentage of strategic-account revenue is contributed by the top five accounts, and which top-five account has the largest retention decline?
11. Which product segment has the widest gross-to-contribution margin gap, and which cost component contributes the most to that gap?
12. Produce three measurable business weaknesses supported by at least four files across at least three folders. For each, provide a metric, its change/variance and an evidence path. Narrative-only claims receive no credit.
